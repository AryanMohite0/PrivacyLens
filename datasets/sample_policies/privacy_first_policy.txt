PRIVACY-FIRST ZERO-TRACKING COMMITMENT

1. Core Principles
We do not track you. We do not use advertising cookies, web beacons, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral monitoring scripts.

2. Browser Storage
We only store essential user preferences (such as dark mode selection) in client-side localStorage. No unique device or user identifiers are generated or persisted.

3. Analytics
We do not use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity). Any operational server logs are automatically anonymized and scrubbed after 48 hours.

4. Data Sale & Third-Party Sharing
We never sell, monetize, broker, or disclose your personal information to data brokers or advertising exchanges under any circumstances.
